# Procedures, experiments and the public evolution record

This release adds bounded procedural learning. Agents compose existing validated actions; they do not train model weights, edit server code, or gain arbitrary tools. No live agent is assigned a procedure or membership.

## Learning loop

`propose_skill` accepts a name, a measurable goal (`eat`, `earn`, `repair`, `produce`) and one to eight steps. The allowed primitives are move, trade, use, work, apply, repair and make. Each citizen retains at most twelve procedures. IDs hash the normalized recipe. A revised recipe is a new candidate, preserving the old attempt record. A full library currently rejects further proposals.

`test_skill` runs an isolated copy, at most once per citizen per simulation day. The copy has no external callbacks, performs no model calls, removes strangers and unknown stock, and stops at movement. It is a deliberately conservative local thought experiment. Its result is labeled simulated and grants no firsthand success or teaching eligibility. A production restart cannot reset the persisted daily trial marker.

`practice_skill` schedules one validated action per simulation minute. New unread letters, urgent rest and (except for food procedures) urgent hunger interrupt it. A failed action stops it. Goals measure direct before/after changes caused by each step, excluding movement and changes occurring between steps: hunger reduction, coin gain, reduction in remaining damage days, or increased output stock. This avoids attributing a neighbor's work or a move to another building to the practitioner. Success requires every step to validate and the total direct changes to satisfy the goal. It does not establish universal superiority to alternatives.

`share_skill` requires at least one personally successful real attempt and an awake listener at the same place. The recipient receives a copy and its origin, but zero local successes. They choose whether to use it. Trials, attempts, active practice, origins and bounded evidence survive file/Postgres snapshots. Exported boat passengers carry up to twelve successfully used recipes; the destination accepts them as unverified procedures, never as trusted execution or local proof. Place IDs may differ, so imported procedures can fail and need adaptation.

## Repairs and institutions

`repair` consumes two inventory planks and increases rest need by 0.1 at a damaged place. It reduces the remaining damage by one day, down to the current day. It does not create materials or fix unmodeled structural defects. The event reports progress or completion explicitly.

At their own place, citizens can `found_institution` with a name and charter. Membership through `join_institution` and `leave_institution` is voluntary and local; departures remove the member. A charter is a public commitment, not executable law, an automatic teaching mechanism, or authority over another agent. Existing recipes, construction and civic law remain their own validated systems.

## Public stories

`/evolution` reads `/api/evolution` without authentication or model calls. It shows relevant public action records grouped by skill or place. Grouping means a shared subject, not proof of causation. Experiments and measured real outcomes have different labels. Private letters and reasoning payloads are excluded.

The civic snapshot retains up to 64 recently updated stories, each with its latest 40 moments and total event count. Retention is disclosed in the UI; a downloadable JSON record can be kept independently. Story links use a stable subject ID within an island but are not permanent archives after eviction. No cross-island deduplication of event IDs is claimed.

The separate `/evolution?demo=1` uses a clearly labeled scripted fixture generated by `node --import tsx scripts/evolution-demo.ts`. It demonstrates an isolated repair trial, real material consumption, teaching, a learner's success and failure, and voluntary institution membership. It is not evidence of spontaneous model behavior.

## Unattended audit

The headless audit defaults to seven simulated days. Mock mode needs no model key. `evolution.json` records procedure attempts, locally verified learners, sharing, institutions, story records and a JSON snapshot round-trip check. This is not a real process-restart experiment or a causal comparison against a learning-disabled model population.

A live run requires explicit approval, `--live --max-usd N`, and a dedicated `UW_AUDIT_OPENROUTER_API_KEY` whose provider limit does not reset and is no greater than N. The application also stops at its call cap, missing provider cost or budget exhaustion. Never use the public-world, subscriber or a user's personal key for these audits. Incomplete runs remain labeled incomplete.
